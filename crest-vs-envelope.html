Crest vs Envelope

One wave packet, two speeds. The crests move at the phase velocity v_p = ω/k. The envelope moves at the group velocity v_g = dω/dk. Watch the blue crest slide through the green envelope.

Speeds

Make them equal and the crests stop moving inside the envelope: no dispersion.

Only the envelope carries energy and information. A crest is just a point of equal phase, so it may move faster or slower than the signal.

Speeds in units of c, slowed down for the eye; the carrier wavelength is one grid step. In silica at 1550 nm v_p = c/1.444 and v_g = c/1.463, only 1.3% apart, so the difference here is exaggerated.
