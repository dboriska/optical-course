Optical Communication Labs

Interactive demonstrations for the course lectures. They run in any browser, on a laptop or a phone.

Lecture 02 · Optical Fibers

Snell's Law Lab Change the angle and the refractive indices and watch rays and waves refract, reach the critical angle and turn into total internal reflection. Includes Fresnel reflectance. Acceptance Cone and NA Lab Aim a ray at the fiber face and change n₁, Δ and the core diameter. See when the ray is guided or escapes, and the acceptance cone in 3D. Graded-Index Ray Race Rays race through a step-index and a graded-index fiber, with the core colored by the speed of light. See why the parabolic profile keeps the pulse together.
