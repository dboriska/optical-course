Chromatic Dispersion

Crest vs Envelope One wave packet, two speeds: the crests move at the phase velocity and the envelope at the group velocity. Phase vs Group Velocity Add two waves of slightly different frequency and watch the beats. See vp and vg on the ω–k diagram and why they differ when there is dispersion. Phase and Group Velocity Lab The carrier moves at c/n, the envelope that carries the data at c/ng. Change the wavelength and see the group index and the delay of a fiber of length L. Pulse Spreading Lab Each frequency in the pulse travels at its own group velocity, so the pulse spreads by ΔT = |D|·L·Δλ. Launch a pulse and watch neighbouring bits start to overlap. Rate and Reach Map How far can each fiber carry a given bit rate? Compare the modal-dispersion limit of multimode fiber with the chromatic-dispersion limit of single-mode fiber, and drag the dot to place your link.
