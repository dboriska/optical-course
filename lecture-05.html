Pulse Dispersion and Chirp

Gaussian Pulse Dispersion in Fiber Drag the distance along the fiber and watch a Gaussian pulse spread while its energy stays the same. Find the dispersion length LD, compare two pulse widths, color the pulse by its chirp, and send a stream of bits to see where they merge. Chirp Lab Give the pulse an initial chirp C₀ and see when it first compresses and when it broadens, with the instantaneous frequency across the pulse. Two-Fiber Pulse Lab Send a pulse through standard fiber and then dispersion-compensating fiber. Set the pulse width and each fiber's dispersion and length, and swap the order of the fibers. DCF and DSP Lab Undo the fiber's dispersion in two ways: optically with a dispersion-compensating fiber, or electronically with digital signal processing in a coherent receiver. Link Length Calculator What limits the link, loss or dispersion? Set the bit rate, the source and the fiber and find the longest link you can build. Undo the Fiber A game: a pulse has crossed an unknown length of fiber. Choose the compensating fiber to bring it back to its original width. Signal Bandwidth Lab Pick a sine, square, triangle or Gaussian signal from 1 MHz to 50 GHz and see its FFT spectrum, its bandwidth, what a low-pass filter leaves of it, and how little of the 193 THz optical carrier it occupies.
