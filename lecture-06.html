Semiconductor Lasers

Einstein's Three Processes Watch atoms absorb, emit spontaneously and emit by stimulated emission, and see when stimulated emission wins. Three Processes Animation Absorption, spontaneous emission and stimulated emission, one after another on the energy-level diagram. Direct vs Indirect Band Gap Why GaAs and InP emit light and silicon does not: the photon carries no momentum, so the transition must be vertical in k. III-V Materials Lab Choose the InGaAsP composition lattice-matched to InP and see the band gap and emission wavelength, from 1310 to 1550 nm. Semiconductor Gain Lab Raise the carrier density and watch the gain spectrum go from absorption through transparency to gain. Laser Cavity Lab Fabry–Perot cavity modes, threshold gain, mirror loss and gain clamping: which modes lase as the pump rises. Bragg Grating Animation How a periodic grating reflects only the Bragg wavelength, the idea behind the single-mode DFB laser.
