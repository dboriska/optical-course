Graded-Index Ray Race

The core is colored by the speed of light in the glass: slow (high index) at the center, fast (low index) near the edge. A pulse launches rays at several angles at the same moment. In the step-index fiber the steep rays fall behind. In the graded-index fiber they take longer paths through faster glass and arrive together. The panel below shows where each ray turns back.

slower lightfaster light
Along a ray β = n(x) cos θz stays the same. The ray turns back where n(x) = β. If β < n₂ there is no such point inside the core and the ray escapes.
