Spot Size Lab

The fundamental mode LP₀₁ (HE₁₁) of a step-index fiber is close to a Gaussian, I(ρ) ∝ exp(−2ρ²/w²), with the spot size w/a ≈ 0.65 + 1.619 V^−3/2 + 2.879 V^−6. Lower V and watch the mode spread into the cladding.

The sweep shrinks and grows the core at a fixed λ.

Exact mode: weakly guiding LP₀₁ field, J₀(uρ/a) in the core and K₀(wρ/a) in the cladding, from u·J₁(u)/J₀(u) = w·K₁(w)/K₀(w). The exact A_eff = (∫I dA)² / ∫I² dA, and the overlap is the fraction of power that a Gaussian beam with the fitted w couples into the mode. The fit (Marcuse, 1977) is meant for 1.2 < V < 2.4; the exact best Gaussian (dots on the w/a chart) is the one with the highest overlap. Δ = (n₁² − n₂²)/(2n₁²).
