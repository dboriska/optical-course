Phase and Group Velocity Lab

The carrier crests move at the phase velocity v_p = c/n; the envelope, which carries the data, moves at the group velocity v_g = 1/β₁ = c/n_g, with n_g = n − λ dn/dλ. A fiber of length L delays the pulse by T = L n_g / c.

Pure silica, Sellmeier coefficients B = 0.6962, 0.4079, 0.8975 and λⱼ = 68.4 nm, 116.2 nm, 9.896 µm. The guided mode of a real fiber has a slightly different n_eff and n_g, but the picture is the same. In the animation the carrier wavelength is drawn about a hundred thousand times longer and the wave is slowed down; only the ratio v_p / v_g matters. "Crests passing the envelope" is the number of carrier periods by which the crests outrun the envelope over the length L, L(n_g − n)/λ.
