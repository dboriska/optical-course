16 RZ Gaussian bits at 40 Gb/s (FWHM 10 ps), propagated exactly with an FFT. Fiber: D = 17 ps/(nm·km) at 1550 nm, slope 0.058 ps/(nm²·km), length 30–120 km. DCF: D = −100 ps/(nm·km) at 1550 nm with half of the slope needed to match the fiber. Level 2 channels: 1535 and 1560 nm. Tolerance ±63 ps/nm (±1000 ps/nm × (10/40)²). Score per round: 100 points if the worst residual is within a quarter of the tolerance, falling to 0 at three times the tolerance. Eye opening: lowest "1" minus highest "0" at the bit centers, relative to the sent pulse peak.
