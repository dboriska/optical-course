III-V Materials Lab

A laser's wavelength is set by the band gap of its active layer, λ ≈ 1.24/Eg. Mixing III-V compounds tunes the gap, but every layer must also grow on a substrate with almost the same lattice constant (within about 0.1%). Pick an alloy, change its composition, and find which alloys reach the fiber windows while staying lattice-matched.
