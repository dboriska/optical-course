Steady state of the two rate equations of slide 67, solved exactly including spontaneous emission into the lasing mode, R_sp = β_sp N/τ_e with β_sp = 10⁻⁴. InGaAsP laser at 1550 nm: cavity 250 × 2 × 0.2 µm, Γ = 0.3, n_g = 3.4, differential gain 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁶ cm², transparency 10¹⁸ cm⁻³ (N₀ = 10⁸ electrons), τ_e = 2.2 ns, all injected electrons reach the active layer. Photon lifetime 1/τ_p = v_g(α_int + α_mir) with α_mir = (1/L) ln(1/R). Output from one facet: P_out = ½ ħω v_g α_mir P. Temperature enters through I_th ∝ e^(T/T₀): N₀ grows and the gain constant shrinks by the same factor, so the slope stays fixed (real lasers also lose some slope when hot). η_d = (q/ħω) dP_out/dI per facet, η_ext = (P_out/ħω)/(I/q), η_tot = P_out/(IV) with V = ħω/q + I · 5 Ω.
