Three Ways Light Meets Electrons

Absorption, spontaneous emission and stimulated emission between two levels E₁ and E₂ (slide 31). Each transition absorbs or emits one photon of energy hν = E₂ − E₁.

ρ(ν): photon energy density per unit frequency. N₁, N₂: densities of electrons in the lower and upper levels. B₁₂, B₂₁ and A₂₁ are the Einstein coefficients; with equal degeneracies B₁₂ = B₂₁ and A₂₁/B₂₁ = 8πhν³n³/c³ (slide 33).
