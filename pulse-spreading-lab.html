Pulse Spreading Lab

A pulse is a band of frequencies, and in a single-mode fiber each one travels at its own group velocity. The spread of delays is ΔT = |β₂| L Δω = |D| L Δλ, so the pulse broadens while its spectrum stays the same. Launch a pulse and watch neighboring bits start to overlap.

Gaussian input pulse at λ = 1550 nm, power ∝ exp(−t²/T₀²), FWHM = 1.665 T₀. The fiber multiplies every frequency component by exp(j β₂ L Δω²/2); the output is shown in a frame that moves with the group delay. β₂ = −λ² D / (2πc), so D = 17 ps/(nm·km) gives β₂ ≈ −21.7 ps²/km. In the bit stream the fields of neighboring pulses add before the detector squares them. "Energy in its bit slot" is the share of one pulse's energy inside its own slot of length 1/B; the target is 95%. Group index n_g = 1.468.
