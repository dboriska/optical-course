Multimode fibers (Lecture 2, ray optics, ΔT < 1/B): step index ΔT = L n₁² Δ / (c n₂), so B·L < n₂c / (n₁²Δ); parabolic graded index ΔT = L n₁ Δ² / (2c), so B·L < 2c / (n₁Δ²). Single-mode fibers: a Gaussian pulse of the best input width, with at least 95% of its energy inside its bit slot, gives B < 1 / (4√(|β₂| L)). β₂ = −λ² D / (2πc) at 1550 nm. The two criteria differ a little, so compare the slopes more than the exact crossing points. Loss, the source spectrum, and dispersion compensation are not included.
